# Your Tutor — Bath Year 1

Live: **https://matthieu-ide.github.io/bath-study/**

Two interactive study apps for University of Bath first-year maths, built around the actual
lecture notes:

| | |
|---|---|
| [Probability & Statistics 1A](https://matthieu-ide.github.io/bath-study/probability/) | 98 pages decoded line by line, 34 animations, 30 labs, unlimited generated questions |
| [Algebra 1A](https://matthieu-ide.github.io/bath-study/algebra/) | 67 pages decoded line by line, 17 animations, 21 labs, complex numbers through determinants |

## The notes

The lecture notes ship with each app, shared with their authors' permission for non-commercial
study. Open a site and the PDF is simply there — nothing to set up. If it ever fails to load
(patchy connection mid-download) the app offers to use a copy you pick yourself, which it then
keeps in that browser.

Progress, mistakes and the flashcard schedule live in browser storage, so use the same browser
and profile to keep them. The two apps use separate databases and do not interfere. There are
no accounts and no tracking; everything runs on your device.

## What's in this repository

The built static sites and the two sets of lecture notes. Source for the apps lives in the
`bath-smartstudy` and `bath-algebra` projects.
